Before you come

Getting there

Address

The Pacific Pavilion
1855 Ocean Front Walk
Santa Monica, CA 90401

Parking

Pier deck lot, $18 flat, card only. Full by nine on Sunday, so come early or come by train.

Transit

Metro E Line to Downtown Santa Monica, then four blocks west down Colorado to the pier ramp.

Access

All four halls step-free by the boardwalk ramp. ASL on every main stage panel, quiet room all hours.

Can I buy a single day?

General admission is sold by the day, or both days for a little less than two singles. Gold and Founders are both-day passes only.

What do the activities cost?

Nothing. Every game on the floor is included with any pass. Autographs, photo ops and food are the only things charged separately.

Cosplay and prop rules?

Costumes yes, functional weapons no. Props must be obviously fake and peace-bonded at the gate. Masks come off once for badge check and go straight back on.

Age limit?

Under sixteens go free with a paying adult and stay with that adult. The bars card at the door on Monday evening.

What if it rains?

Everything but the boardwalk deck is under the pavilion roof. If the weather turns, the food carts move inside and the opening bell moves to the main stage.
